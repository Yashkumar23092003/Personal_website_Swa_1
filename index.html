    ON YOUR BIRTHDAY, WITH ALL MY LOVE

    Dear Swati,

    Happy birthday, my lovely Swati. I wanted to make you something you could come back to whenever you need a smile, so I gathered a few of my favourite moments with you.

    My wish for you this year is that you feel loved, find time for the things that make you happy, and feel proud of the person you’re becoming. I’ll be cheering for you, in the big moments and the quiet ones too.

    Looking at these pictures, I keep thinking how lovely it is that, in this big, busy world, I get to sit next to you.

    I hope there are so many more photographs. More silly smiles. More days that don’t seem extraordinary until we look back and realise they were.

    And on the days you need a little reminder: you are so loved. I hope this little corner of the world makes you feel it.

    I may not always manage something this elaborate, but I hope you never mistake a quieter gesture for less love. I hope we keep our spark in the little things too, making time, checking in, and choosing each other on ordinary days when we are busy but we know we are their.

    I’m grateful to Krishna for bringing us together. I hope we both remember Him in our happy moments too, and thank Him for the little joys we get to share.May we keep Him at the centre of our love, and grow closer to Him as we grow closer to each other.

    Here’s to your next year, and all the pages still unwritten.
    With love, always ♡
